How movement works

  1. Choose the territory you want to move troops from.
  2. Choose an adjacent territory controlled by the same side.
  3. Enter how many eligible troop units should move.

Troops can move once per turn. Units that arrive in a territory are added to its total, but they do not become movable again until the next turn.
